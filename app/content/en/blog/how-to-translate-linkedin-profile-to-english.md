# How to translate a LinkedIn profile to English

When users search this phrase, they usually do not want abstract branding advice. They want a practical process that helps them move section by section.

## A useful article outline

1. Start with the headline because it shapes first impressions.
2. Rewrite the About section for clarity instead of literal translation.
3. Convert experience bullets into concise, metric-friendly English.

## Important framing

The article should explain that LinkedIn translation is not the same as translating a legal document. The goal is readability, relevance, and recruiter comprehension rather than literal one-to-one mapping.

## Best product bridge

After the tutorial has done its job, link directly to the dedicated profile translation page. That route matches the user's next action better than sending them back to a generic homepage section.
