# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how **NB2 Studio** collects, uses, and protects your information.

NB2 Studio is an independent product and is **not affiliated with Google**.

## 2. Information We Collect

## 3. How We Use Information

- Prevent abuse, fraud, and policy violations

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers (hosting, analytics, payments) strictly for service delivery and compliance.

## 5. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for service operations, legal compliance, and security.
